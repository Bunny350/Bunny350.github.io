Sponsorship policy

This lists the policy about sponsoring with Oitswilliam, using Oitswilliam properly, the strategies and limitations. You may agree to this policy before requesting for the sponsorship. You may not use such services if you do not agree with this policy.

Oitswilliam Pang Passivepromo and Activepromo are the two official types of sponsor service. Passivepromo only applies to places while Activepromo applies to hardware products (including remix of OIWP PCBs and non-OIWP products) and places.

Passivepromo will be unlocked if Oitswilliam Pang reaches 1k followers in both Facebook account, Facebook page and Instagram (at least or average 500 in a platform or per platform respectively), or 5k subscribers on YouTube, which is too far but is needed to reach.

Activepromo will be available if Oitswilliam Pang reaches 10k followers in either Facebook account / Facebook page, or Instagram (followers in other platforms do not count).

Other platforms include GitHub, Reddit, Steam, X and Xbox, former includes Planet Minecraft and DeviantArt. Oitswilliam will not track these until further announcement.

Sponsors whose sponsoring with Oitswilliam Pang before Oitswilliam reach 40k followers in both Facebook page and Instagram, or 20k subscribers on YouTube will receive Early Sponsor badge on and will be on the top of this Sponsored products page.

Sponsors who's no longer sponsoring with Oitswilliam will be put on the former sponsors section.

List of allowed and disallowed products

Passivepromo only apply to places. Passivepromo may only apply to places that are not for illegal activities. Passivepromo may not be applicable to bars, music shows or outer space. You may acknowledge that Oitswilliam may bring the innovations of his idea to your place if possible.
