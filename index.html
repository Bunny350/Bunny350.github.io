Who is Oitswilliam Pang?

Oitswilliam Pang (real name: Pang William) is a maker, or actually, banger CAD designer who makes projects that pushes boundaries and mostly free and open to obtain, with interfaces that are intuitive and brings joy.

He became listed in November 2012 with debut of his Facebook account, hardware is his dream. Now he has 2 main product categories, official products and mods for other community.

Oitswilliam will be the leader of reshaping the UX on 3D printers.

He is a Minecrafter with the respective gamer-tags for Java and Bedrock, Bunny_350 and Bunny350, but now in part-time.

In case you don't know, he is also a concept artist, because he sometimes lose his minds trying to design it through CAD, isn't that true? And one person-operation is no longer enough, so Oitswilliam decide to invite the people around the industry, even if different, not generative AI.
