Oitswilliam Pang Minecraft Maps and Legacy Projects Policies

Effective on June 15, 2026.

This shows lists of policies that apply to Maps and legacy projects for Minecraft that are made by Oitswilliam Pang. You may agree to this policy in order to let you download maps or legacy projects made by Oitswilliam.

For policies apply to Sheer Dedicated Server, you may visit SDS Usage Policy.

Definitions

There are list of definitions for this policy.
  1. Obtaining / downloading is a procedure when downloading Oitswilliam's Minecraft Maps or legacy projects.
  2. Legacy projects are formerly Command Creations during Bunny 350 days.
  3. Bunny 350 is the name for Oitswilliam Pang before June 17 of 2022.
  4. Modifying is an action that basically means changing things.
  5. Sharing is an action that means to distribute across the place.
  6. Used Oitswilliam Pang creations means that any of Oitswilliam Pang creations that were used by someone else after Oitswilliam Pang distributed. This includes is as simple as just visiting the world.
  7. Unauthorized copies means that the copy of the world was distributed with links different from the official's.
  8. Legacy projects are shared from Oitswilliam's Dropbox drive, however later versions may be shared from Oitswilliam's Google Drive or Planet Minecraft.

Obtaining / Downloading

It's best to check how the project works before download, since some of them might corrupt or damage itself if you do something randomly.

In some maps, if you are in normal copy of the legacy project, you are not possible to change game modes.

Maps created by Oitswilliam Pang may be copyrighted with the proprietary license. In legacy and sometimes obsolete projects that were not updated since or after June 17 of 2022, Oitswilliam Pang is formerly known as Bunny 350.

Licensing for legacy projects are proprietary and you must give them an official link to the official project.

Modifying

Legacy projects can be fixed, patched or modified by the followers of Oitswilliam Pang. You must be a follower of Oitswilliam Pang on Facebook, Instagram, X or subscriber on YouTube and request for a modifiable copy of such map through the support page in which we will provide the projects with the ownership detection disabled. You may not distribute such projects to the public.

Sharing

You can share with the official link provided, it was only modified by our trusted contributors. Avoid sharing links that are not official, or even just redirects of the official download link.

Avoid sharing used Oitswilliam Pang creations. Some legacy creations has proprietary licensing system related to anti-piracy, such as ownership detection, to prevent unauthorized copies of these world.

List of legacy projects listed as obsolete

  • RabbitBot - A player controlled rabbit with just command controls
    • Introduced in 2015 and released in 2016, as Oitswilliam's first step to publicly available command creations.
    • Officially discontinued and marked development obsolete in 2023. Although users can still get it as scraps of command lines, it works until versions later JE 1.9.4.
